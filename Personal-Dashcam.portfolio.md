# DashcamIQ

**Computer-vision system that detects driving anomalies in paired dashcam footage and assigns a per-trip driver safety score, with three model tiers compared head-to-head.**

## What it does

Detects eight anomaly classes across paired front/rear dashcam clips — hard braking, near-misses, lane departure, traffic violations, tailgating, harsh cornering, aggressive lane changes, and distracted driving — and rolls them into a 0–100 per-trip score with an A–F grade. Per-incident natural-language explanations are generated by Claude from the structured detection metadata.

## Data

Front/rear MP4 clips stored on Cloudflare R2 and paired by filename timestamp within a 5-second tolerance. Frames are streamed via OpenCV (lazy generator) and metadata is read with ffprobe. Hand-labeled through an in-app admin queue: 626 labeled clips → 35,598 sliding windows at a 2.6% anomaly rate. Stratified train/test split locked in `data/splits.json`.

## Models

- **Naive baseline** — Farneback dense optical flow with magnitude and variance thresholding over 30-frame windows. No training.
- **Classical ML** — 19 hand-crafted features (optical-flow stats, motion-direction variance, motion blur via Laplacian, edge-density change, temporal spike patterns) feed an XGBoost binary classifier. Trained with 5-fold CV; Random Forest trained alongside for comparison.
- **Deep learning** — YOLOv8s per-frame detection (vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists, traffic lights, stop signs) produces 13-dim per-frame vectors over 30-frame sliding windows with 50% overlap, fed into a bidirectional LSTM with learned attention pooling (best — val AUC 0.88, F1 0.35) and a 2-layer Transformer encoder (val AUC 0.78, F1 0.18) for comparison.

## Training

Deep-learning models are trained with focal loss + `pos_weight`, weighted random oversampling of anomaly windows, AdamW, CosineAnnealingLR, and early stopping on validation F1 (patience 10). The decision threshold is optimized automatically on the validation set and saved with the checkpoint. Gaussian-noise feature augmentation is applied during training. A training-size sensitivity experiment retrains the classical model at 10/25/50/75/100% of the train split across 5 seeds.

## Stack

PyTorch · Ultralytics YOLOv8 · OpenCV · XGBoost · scikit-learn · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · SQLAlchemy + Alembic · Celery + Redis · Cloudflare R2 · Next.js · Anthropic Claude API.
